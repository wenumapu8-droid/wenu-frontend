Initial jewelry · Piercing service

These are the options. All of them.

Every fresh piercing starts in implant-grade titanium — included in the fee. Solid 14kt gold is the only upgrade. Nothing else goes into a new piercing.

  • Ti-6Al-4V ELI · ASTM F-136
  • Factory-sterilised (EtO)
  • Sealed, lot & date
  • Titanium included

02

Solid 14kt gold

Upgrade

The post stays titanium — only the end that shows is gold. Six ends, three prices. Added to the fee.

  • Tier I

    +$60

    CZ prong pin · Opal cabochon

  • Tier II

    +$80

    Gold disk · Hammered disk

  • Tier III

    +$120

    Ceres top · Trinity top

  • Tier I · +$60

    Gold CZ prong pin

    14kt yellow gold · 1.5mm · clear CZ

    The smallest light you can wear. Reads as a single point of white.

  • Tier I · +$60

    Gold opal cabochon

    14kt yellow gold · 2mm · opal

    A dome, not a facet. It shifts colour with the angle of the room.

  • Tier II · +$80

    Gold disk

    14kt yellow gold · 2.5mm

    Plain, polished, absolute. The end that never goes out of date.

  • Tier II · +$80

    Hammered disk

    14kt yellow gold · 3mm

    Struck by hand so the surface holds light unevenly, like water.

  • Tier III · +$120

    Ceres top

    14kt yellow gold · pear + round amethyst

    A drop and a point above it. Worn vertically, it lengthens the ear.

  • Tier III · +$120

    Trinity top

    14kt yellow gold · three stones

    Three stones in a trefoil. The most light on the smallest footprint.
