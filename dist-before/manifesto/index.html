The body is sacred territory — the Wenu Mapu manifesto

A visionary journey through Mapuche cosmology: Wenu Mapu, the sky-world and its sun, the ancestors' road of stars, the moon, Nag Mapu the living earth, descending through the four forces — fire, wind, water, stone — to the kultrún as a map of the universe, the cosmic double helix at the center, a toroidal net of eyes that watches back, and Miñche Mapu — the whirlpool that returns everything to the still center, to nothing, to begin again.

A visual manifesto: a DNA double-helix descends from Earth at the top, out through scales — atom, seed, cell, the Source, planets, sun, galaxy — and pours into a black hole at the bottom. Gold and cyan strands, organic and cybernetic, on obsidian.

Wenu Mapu — the manifesto

The body is
sacred territory

An awakening · from the sky-world to the self

The source

Everything begins
as light

The Mapuche name the world above Wenu Mapu — the land of light, where the ancestors dwell. A single radiance chose to become many. You are one of the ways it learned to see itself.

The wandering fires

The fire that lit the stars
sleeps inside your bones

The Milky Way is Rüpü — the luminous road the ancestors walk. Iron, gold, calcium: every atom in you was forged along it. You are not watching the cosmos. You are the cosmos, remembering itself.

The moon

She who moves the sea
moves the blood

Küyen never touches you, yet you answer her. Every rhythm in the body is a smaller echo of a larger sky. Küyen ñi az

The earth

A blue seed
suspended in the dark

Nag Mapu — the land here, where we stand. Water, breath, soil and stone practiced making bodies long before they made ours. We are the earth, learning to walk and to wonder.

The threshold

Now we turn inward,
toward the world below

To descend into the body is to travel toward Miñche Mapu, the land beneath — through the four forces from which every living thing is woven.

Kütral · Kürüf · Ko · Kura

Plasma · fire

Before form,
there was only fire

Kütral — the state of stars and lightning. Pure charge, undecided, still becoming. The first breath of creation, still glowing in the deep.

Gas · wind

Then the fire cooled
into breath

Kürüf — the wind, the air, the spirit that fills the lungs and moves the world. To breathe is to remember you were once wind.

Liquid · water

Breath became water,
water became blood

Ko — water. The body is mostly ocean. What flows through the rivers flows through you: the same water, only borrowed for a lifetime.

Solid · stone

And water became
bone, stone, metal

Kura — stone. Matter finally holds a shape. Here adornment is born: form made sacred, the cosmos choosing to wear a face.

The kultrún

The drum is a map
of the whole universe

Meli Witran Mapu — the four places, oriented from the east: Puel, Willi, Lafken, Pikun. The kultrún holds the three worlds at once. To adorn the body is to carry the cosmos, oriented. Meli Witran Mapu

The spiral within

At the center of the body:
the same spiral as the sky

The strand that writes every living thing turns like a galaxy, coils like a serpent, rises like smoke. The ancestors read the spiral in the stars before we found it in the cell.

The net of eyes

You were never
watching alone

Itrofill Mongen — all life, whole and interwoven. The Ngen — spirits of water, forest and stone — are watching. The cosmos looks back at you through a thousand eyes.

The source, again

All that spirals out
spirals home

The whirlpool takes the sun, the stars, the body, the name — everything returns to the still center, the great mystery. And from the nothing, it all begins again. Wenu mapu ñi kimün
