## 2. Brand promise

To every customer:

1. **The piece is real.** Hand-forged in Truckee. No drop-ship, no white-label, no mass production.
2. **The materials are named, sourced, and safe.** Implant-grade titanium (ASTM F-136), sterling silver 950, 14k gold, Atacama meteorite, walnut wood, brass.
3. **The process is patient.** Small batches. Some pieces are one-of-a-kind. Commissions take six months.
4. **The voice is honest.** No magical claims. No festival language. No invented "tribal" patterns.
5. **The relationship is direct.** You email the maker (marimari@). The maker replies. No middlemen.

---

## 3. What Wenu Mapu sells (beyond the object)

- **Permanence on the body.** Body jewelry is worn daily, often for years. Wenu Mapu is sold as something you live with.
- **A defensible cosmology.** Mapuche-rooted, Truckee-grounded. Not interchangeable with any boho or generic ritual brand.
- **Material authority.** Each material is named with a canonical phrase (BRAND-DNA §4). The customer learns alongside the brand.
- **Slow consumption as luxury.** Limited drops + named author series + commissions. The opposite of fast jewelry.
- **A one-on-one relationship.** Founder replies. Local delivery is hand-delivered. Custom orders are co-designed.

## 8. Pricing / value perception

- **No discounts.** Premium niche brands earn margin by holding the line. (Aesop, Maria Tash, BVLA, Buddha Jewelry — none discount publicly.)
- **No "Sale" page.** When clearance happens, name it "VAULT" (Ask & Embla pattern) — communicates archive, not desperation.
- **No anchor-price gimmicks.** Don't show fake "was $150 / now $120" unless it's literally true and recent.
- **Free shipping over $150** — already in copy. Holds the line.
- **Free local delivery** — never charge the local customer. The relationship is worth more than the shipping label cost.
- **Commission deposits** — 50% non-refundable on confirmation. Buyer signals seriousness; founder's time is protected.
- **Currency: USD only** for the US market. Don't show CLP / EUR until international scaling.

## 9. Limited drop strategy

- **Cadence:** 4–6 drops per year, mapped to the Mapuche calendar:
  - **Wiñoy Xipantu** (Mapuche New Year, June 24)
  - **Pewu** (Spring equinox, late September)
  - **Walüng** (Summer solstice, December 21)
  - **Rimu** (Autumn equinox, March 21)
  - + 1–2 named "between" drops on artistic milestones (e.g., a meteorite arrival, a specific stone shipment)
- **Drop size:** 3–8 pieces per drop. Some pieces single-edition (one-of-one), some pairs.
- **Announcement:** The Wenu Mapu List gets the drop 24–48 hours before the catalog goes live.
- **Scarcity is real, not theatrical.** No "fake countdowns." When the piece is gone, it's gone.
- **Drop landing page:** `/drops/<event-slug>` (e.g., `/drops/winoy-xipantu-2026`). Generated from a template (Codex Task — deferred). Permanent URL acts as archive after the drop closes.

This rhythm + the founder's voice in the announcement is the single most important sales lever after the trust block.

## 10. Local appointment + delivery strategy

The most undervalued lever today.

- **Service area:** Truckee, Kings Beach, Tahoe Vista, and nearby North Lake Tahoe / Truckee airport area.
- **Appointments:** by request only. Selected pieces brought to a private guest space (not "home display"); typical visit 30–60 minutes; unhurried.
- **Local delivery:** free, within 24–72 hours of payment. Hand-delivered.
- **Quiet upgrade play:** when delivering, offer to bring 1–2 additional pieces in the same material for the customer to consider in person. ("Would you like to see two other pieces in titanium during the drop-off?") Pure relationship; no pressure.
- **Communication:** marimari@ for booking; phone (408) 500-6211 for confirmation; @wenu__mapu for visual questions.
- **Privacy:** appointment locations confirmed only after booking; never published.

## 11. Custom orders strategy

- **Cadence:** 6–10 commissions per year (small, for quality control).
- **Lead time:** 6 months from accepted inquiry to delivery.
- **Pricing band:** $400–$2,500+ depending on material + complexity.
- **Process:** inquiry → 5-day reply → concept call → sketch + quote → 50% deposit → forging → delivery. (Already in copy.)
- **Qualification questions** at the form stage filter unserious inquiries.
- **3 worked-example commissions** on `/custom-orders` page — anonymized photos + brief story — proves the process is real.
- **Auto-reply within seconds** sets the relationship temperature.
- **Single founder voice** in every reply. No "support team" pretense.

## 13. Editorial / content strategy (the moat)

- **/journal** as an editorial publication, not a blog.
- **Six founding entries** map to brand pillars:
  1. What is Vacamuerta meteorite?
  2. The four cardinal forces.
  3. Why we forge in Truckee.
  4. Forging the meteorite.
  5. Aftercare — the first 90 days.
  6. Reading the body for adornment.
- **Length:** 600–1,200 words per entry.
- **Voice:** ritual, refined, direct, sensory. Per BRAND-DNA + voz-de-marca-real.
- **Cross-link:** every entry has 2 product cards in the tail + a cross-link to a relevant material/collection landing.
- **Email magnet:** every entry has end-of-entry "Join the circle" capture.

The Journal is the email moat (Aesop pattern), the SEO moat (Buddha "Edit" pattern), and the founder voice anchor.
