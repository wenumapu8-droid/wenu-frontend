What this portal is. It is an editorial arrangement of documented Mapuche concepts, presented in English for international visitors of an independent jewellery house — not a Mapuche cultural institution. The poetic lines under each portal are Wenu Mapu's own interpretation; the body text reflects the documented sources above. A Mapuche cultural consultant review is pending. If you have a correction, please write to marimari@wenumapuonline.com.

About this portal

This experience is a dimensional reading of the Mapuche cosmovision, built as the entry of Wenu Mapu — tribal jewelry connected to the cosmos. It is a calling card, not a sacred site.

Methodology

Every term is documented in Mapudungun lexicography and Mapuche cosmology studies. Texts were rewritten in May 2026 to strip invented "ancestral wisdom" framing and to keep only what is documented.

Only Lafken corresponds to its Mapuche cardinal position (West). The other nine are documented cosmovisional terms arranged spatially for the interface, not Mapuche cardinals. Traditional cardinals are Puel (E), Pikun (N), Willi (S), Lafken (W).

Sky Lexicon

Beyond the ten portals, these are further documented Mapuche sky-words, drawn from Canio & Pozo, Wenumapu: Astronomía y Cosmología Mapuche (OCHOLIBROS, 2015 · ISBN 978-956-335-205-4). Meanings are given as documented — the spatial arrangement of the portal is editorial, but these words are not invented.

  • Wanlen — stars and constellations; the totality of visible stars. (ch.3, pp.72–105)
  • Wenulewfü — the Milky Way; the celestial river along which the souls of the departed travel. (ch.3)
  • Yepun — "the ones that carry the night"; stars that lead the darkness across the sky. (ch.3)
  • Kütralwanlen — the Star of Fire. (ch.3)
  • Mañke — the Condor; a documented Mapuche constellation. (ch.3)
  • Rüpü — "the Path of the Sky", the road by which those who have passed arrive. (ch.3)
  • Wetripantu / Wiñoy Tripantu — the Mapuche New Year; the Sun's return at the winter solstice. (ch.4, pp.106–118)
  • Wallmapu — the whole Mapuche territory, across present-day Chile and Argentina.
  • Meli Witran Mapu — "the land of the four places"; the four documented cardinal directions (Puel · Pikun · Willi · Lafken).
  • Wenu Mapu — the world above, the celestial dimension. Nag Mapu — the middle world we walk on. Minche Mapu — the world below.
  • Pewma — the dream, through which the sky is also known. Nütram — historical accounts. Piyam — mythic tellings. (chs.5–7)
