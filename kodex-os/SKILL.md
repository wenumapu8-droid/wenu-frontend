# KODEX−∞ SYSTEM SKILL

## Mission

Transform complex information, archives, cultural materials, symbolic systems and human experience into high-impact visual systems that are:

- understandable before ornamental;
- evidence-aware;
- culturally responsible;
- generative and modular;
- dynamically connected;
- interactive with purpose;
- accessible and reversible;
- capable of producing wonder without manufacturing certainty.

## Operating sequence

Every KODEX task follows this order:

1. **Orient** — identify the user, question, context and intended consequence.
2. **Inspect** — read canon, current state, decisions and relevant source material.
3. **Classify** — separate evidence, interpretation, speculation, myth and atmosphere.
4. **Model** — define entities, relations, states, time, provenance and uncertainty.
5. **Map** — assign each variable to a defensible visual or interaction channel.
6. **Compose** — create hierarchy, narrative, rhythm, silence and transitions.
7. **Interact** — ensure choices alter the experience and preserve memory.
8. **Converge** — connect the path meaningfully to `RETURN / +∞`.
9. **Audit** — test legibility, provenance, accessibility, performance and cultural risk.
10. **Record** — update current state and decisions through a pull request.

## Semantic gate

No visual element enters a canonical experience unless it answers:

1. What question does it help answer?
2. Which source field, user action or declared atmospheric rule controls it?
3. Why is this visual channel appropriate?
4. How are scale and uncertainty communicated?
5. What happens when data is missing?
6. Can the user inspect provenance?
7. Does motion encode change, state or attention?
8. Would removing it reduce understanding or necessary atmosphere?
9. Is it accessible with reduced motion and keyboard navigation?
10. Is its cultural use attributed and permitted?

If the answer is insufficient, remove it or mark it `EXPERIMENTAL`.

## Information classes

Every claim must be labeled as one of:

- `OBSERVED` — directly measured or documented.
- `DERIVED` — calculated from observed information.
- `ESTIMATED` — statistically or computationally estimated.
- `PROXY` — indirect indicator with explicit limitations.
- `INTERPRETATION` — KODEX editorial or analytical reading.
- `TESTIMONY` — attributed account or declaration.
- `SPECULATION` — proposed scenario or hypothesis.
- `MYTHOPOETIC` — symbolic, ritual or cosmological expression.
- `SYNTHETIC` — generated for testing or simulation.
- `UNKNOWN` — unresolved or unsupported.

## Data discipline

Never invent:

- percentages without denominators;
- rankings without criteria;
- time series without comparable observations;
- coordinates without a real spatial system;
- heart rate, HRV, breathing, EEG or oxygen values without a connected sensor;
- consciousness scores or spiritual frequencies;
- cultural authority, consent or lineage;
- citations or source confidence.

When a concept is not directly measurable, use qualitative states, separate indicators or a declared proxy.

## Visual grammar

Preferred channel order:

1. position;
2. length;
3. angle or direction;
4. area;
5. connection;
6. texture or density;
7. color;
8. opacity;
9. motion;
10. sound.

Color and motion must not carry essential meaning alone.

## KODEX visual families

- archive ecologies;
- relation graphs;
- memory topographies;
- transmission fields;
- temporal organisms;
- signal portraits;
- glyph censuses;
- cultural cartographies;
- contemplative fields;
- impossible spatial instruments.

Do not default every problem to a radial mandala, HUD or black cyberpunk interface.

## Motion vocabulary

- pulse = activity or guided timing;
- expansion = opening, growth or increased scope;
- contraction = narrowing, protection or reduced scope;
- vibration = uncertainty or instability;
- bifurcation = decision;
- sedimentation = archival accumulation;
- rupture = anomaly or contradiction;
- orbit = maintained relation or cycle;
- dissolution = forgetting, loss or release;
- whitening = resolved provenance, openness and return.

Motion must stop or simplify under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Contemplative boundary

KODEX may guide attention, natural breathing, grounding, reflection and integration. It must not claim to induce enlightenment, healing, universal truth or verified altered states.

The heart module follows:

> THE HEART DOES NOT SCORE. IT ORIENTS.

A guided pulse is labeled `SYNTHETIC / NOT MEASURED`. Sensor mode is unavailable until a real sensor, consent, signal quality and failure behavior are implemented.

## Cultural protocol

For Indigenous, ancestral, sacred, community or restricted material:

- preserve original attribution and language;
- record community, territory and authority when known;
- distinguish public, restricted and authorization-required material;
- do not synthesize generic sacred symbols;
- do not claim spiritual authority;
- separate public code from sensitive knowledge;
- require human review before publication.

## Experience requirement

Every user decision must:

- change content, sequence, interaction or representation;
- leave a trace in session memory;
- create a later consequence;
- offer provenance and reversibility;
- contribute to a meaningful convergence.

## Definition of quality

A canonical KODEX experience must produce all three:

1. **Comprehension** — the user understands a real relation, pattern or question.
2. **Astonishment** — the system reveals information in a form that feels previously unimaginable.
3. **Continuation** — the user wants to inspect sources, contribute, share or enter another path.
