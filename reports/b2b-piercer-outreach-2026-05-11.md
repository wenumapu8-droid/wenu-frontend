## 2. Email pitch — cold outreach

**From:** Mary Mari `<wholesale@wenumapuonline.com>`
**Reply-To:** marimari@wenumapuonline.com

### Subject lines — A/B/C test

- **A (curiosity / cultural):** `Hand-forged ritual jewelry from the Andes — for [Studio name]`
- **B (gap / opportunity):** `Filling the hand-carved gap Maya left behind`
- **C (direct / craft):** `Mapuche silver + Patagonian fossil-wood — wholesale inquiry`

Use C with established technical buyers (Infinite, Ahimsa, Cold Steel). Use B with curators who knew Maya (So Gold, Adorn, Nine Moons). Use A with newer / luxury-positioned studios (Apollo, 13 Bats, Emerald & Opal).

**Preview text (all variants):**
`Solid 950 silver, 14k gold, implant-grade titanium ASTM F-136, and a material no other studio is carrying.`

## 4. Lookbook digital pitch — 1-page brief

**Format recommendation:** PDF (12–16 pages, ~10–15 MB), hosted at `wenumapuonline.com/wholesale-lookbook.pdf` behind a soft gate (just an email form, no password). Reasons:
- PDF travels — piercer can forward to a partner or print it for the bench.
- Notion is good for active collaboration; cold outreach should land in their inbox without an account-creation step.
- A private Astro page (`/wholesale/lookbook`) is the *second* deliverable — link it after they ask follow-up questions, with embedded video of forging process.

**Contents (in this order):**

1. **Cover** — single hero image of a vacamuerta + silver piece on hand. No logo lockup yet. Caption: one mapudungun phrase + translation.
2. **The brand in 90 words** — who, where, what we work in. No mission-statement waffle.
3. **Materials standards page** — ASTM F-136 titanium, 950 silver fineness, 14k gold composition, vacamuerta provenance (specific region of Patagonia + age range). Piercers read this page first. Get it right.
4. **The mapuche symbol library** — 6–8 symbols (kultrún, choike, ngïrü, etc.) with one-line cultural notes. Position as design vocabulary, not folklore.
5. **The 12 pieces in the opening line** — clean studio photo, dimensions, gauge, weight, material breakdown, retail price, wholesale price (gated separately — see §6). One pad of white space around each.
6. **Process spread (2 pages)** — forging, carving vacamuerta, setting. Documentary photos, not lifestyle.
7. **About Mary Mari** — half page, one photo, dated bio. Personal not corporate.
8. **Wholesale terms summary** — one page (mirror §6).
9. **Back cover** — direct contact, IG handle, wholesale@ alias, phone, response-time commitment (e.g. 24 hrs business days).

**What to leave OUT:** lifestyle photography with models, customer testimonials, stock photos of Patagonia, anything that reads as Etsy / craft-fair.

## 5. Sample kit logistics

**Send 4 pieces (not 3, not 5):** four signals "we curated for you" without looking like a clearance dump. Three feels thin; five feels desperate.

**Recommended kit composition (target retail values per `competitor-analysis-2026-05-11.md` §2):**

| # | Piece | Material | Why it's in the kit | Retail | Wholesale (50% off) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vacamuerta + 950 silver ear weight (mid-statement) | Vacamuerta core, silver hanger | The defensible material story — this is the piece they can't get anywhere else | $245 | $122.50 |
| 2 | 14k gold seam ring with kultrún quadrant engraving | Solid 14k gold | Anchors the kit at fine-jewelry tier — proves we play in BVLA's price range | $310 | $155 |
| 3 | Implant-grade titanium ASTM F-136 threadless end | Titanium + meteorite or silver inlay | Bench-friendly entry piece — what the piercer will personally wear after the meeting | $95 | $47.50 |
| 4 | Plata 950 hanger with choike (rhea) silhouette | Solid 950 silver | Mapuche symbol as design — the storytelling proof | $180 | $90 |

**Kit cost to Wenu (wholesale-cost basis):** ~$415 retail = ~$165–$210 in raw material + labor. Reasonable customer acquisition cost for a wholesale account that should clear $3–8k/yr.

**Terms attached to the kit:**

- **Pre-paid sample kit (recommended for kits #1–3):** Wenu absorbs full cost. Sent with no obligation, no return. The kit IS the marketing budget. Piercer keeps the pieces personally or stocks them — both outcomes are wins.
- **Consignment (offer only if asked):** 60-day window, sale-or-return, piercer pays shipping back if returning. Avoid as default — consignment accounting is a nightmare for a 1-person operation and signals weakness.
- **Sale-or-return on the first opening order (recommended):** if studio commits to an opening order of 8–12 pieces, give them 60 days to sell-through with full return rights on unsold. Removes their risk on the first order without saddling Wenu with consignment ledger work.

**Shipping:**
- Insured USPS Priority or UPS Ground inside US. Tracking + signature on delivery.
- Kit packed in muted unbleached cotton pouch + folded letterpress card with handwritten greeting. The packaging IS part of the sample — they evaluate presentation too.

## 6. Wholesale pricing structure

**Headline:** 50% off MSRP for stocking orders. This is the body-jewelry industry standard for premium hand-made (BVLA, BJO, Tawapa all sit at 45–55% off retail to qualified piercers). 40% is too tight given Wenu's labor cost; 55%+ erodes margin on small batches.

| Term | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Wholesale discount** | 50% off MSRP | Flat. No volume tiering in year 1 — keeps it simple. |
| **Opening order MOQ** | 8 pieces (any mix) OR $600 wholesale | Whichever is higher. Low enough to be a soft yes, high enough that the studio takes display seriously. |
| **Reorder MOQ** | 4 pieces OR $300 wholesale | Lower threshold to keep them topped up. |
| **Payment terms — first order** | 100% pre-paid via wire, Stripe invoice, or card. | No Net terms until 3 successful orders. Protects cash. |
| **Payment terms — established** | Net 30 after 3 paid orders | Standard graduation. |
| **Sale-or-return** | First opening order only, 60 days, unsold returnable in original condition | Removes initial risk. Not offered on reorders. |
| **Lead time** | 2–4 weeks on stock pieces; 6–8 weeks on commissioned vacamuerta items | Stated up front. Piercers plan inventory cycles. |
| **Shipping** | Free on orders $1,000+ wholesale; flat $25 below | Insured + tracked. |
| **Custom commissions** | Available, +30% over wholesale, 50% deposit | Reserved for established accounts (3+ orders in). |
| **Geographic exclusivity** | NOT offered in year 1 | Tempting but premature — locks Wenu out of geo if account underperforms. Re-evaluate at month 12 per top-performing partner. |
| **Returns on defective product** | Full credit or replacement, Wenu pays return shipping | Standard. |
| **Marketing co-op** | Wenu provides hi-res product photography + 2 short forging videos per stocking partner. Partner credits @wenumapuonline on IG posts featuring pieces. | No paid co-op spend in year 1. |
| **Press / story rights** | Partner is free to share Wenu's brand story; Wenu reserves the right to feature partner studio in lookbook + IG with permission. | Mutual. |

**Why no exclusivity yet:** the first 3 wholesale partners will each be in different geos (e.g. one Bay Area, one NYC, one PNW). Geographic exclusivity becomes a real lever in year 2 once Wenu has data on per-account sell-through and can offer it as a retention reward.
