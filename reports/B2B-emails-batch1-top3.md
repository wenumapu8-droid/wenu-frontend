## Send protocol

1. **Verify each email address** on each studio's website Contact page before sending. If the public address is a general info@, that's fine — first reply will route to the owner. If they have a specific wholesale@ or partnerships@ alias, use that instead.

2. **Send Monday or Tuesday between 9-11am studio-local time.** Body-art studios open midday. Avoid Friday or weekend (gets buried).

3. **Send one at a time, 10 minutes apart.** Don't BCC or batch — Gmail's spam filter treats batched B2B sends harder than individual.

4. **Log each send** in the tracking template from `b2b-piercer-outreach-2026-05-11.md` §7. Note: sent date, subject variant, preview text confirmed in inbox view.

5. **Set 5-day follow-up reminder** for any that don't reply. Follow-up email body is ready in §3 of the outreach report.

---

## After first replies — what to do

- **"Send the kit"** → confirm shipping address, pack 4 pieces (Ritual Ring fragment / silver hanger / titanium threadless end / vacamuerta plug), invoice pre-paid (kit cost ~$415 wholesale, see §5 of outreach report), ship USPS Priority with tracking. Total turnaround: 5-7 days.
- **"Send the lookbook first"** → reply same day with PDF attached (lookbook still needs to be produced — see §4 of outreach report for brief).
- **"What's your wholesale pricing?"** → reply with the 50% off MSRP terms, MOQ 8 pieces or $600 opening order, pre-pay first 3 orders then Net 30, no geo exclusivity year 1.
- **"Not for us right now"** → one-line thank you, log as "no — re-engage in 90 days with new pieces."
- **No reply day 5** → send the follow-up.
- **No reply day 10** → silence. Re-engage in 90 days with a piece-announcement, not another follow-up.
