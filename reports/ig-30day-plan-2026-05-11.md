# Instagram — Plan Operativo 30 Dias
### Wenu Mapu · Soft launch new.wenumapuonline.com · Mayo–Junio 2026

---

## 1. Estrategia general

- **Que tipo de cuenta.** Cuenta de marca artesanal premium con voz editorial: no un catálogo de productos, sino un punto de vista sobre el cuerpo como territorio sagrado. Cada pieza tiene una historia de material, proceso o origen. La cuenta no vende directamente — construye deseo y confianza, y deriva a bio link o DM para cerrar.

- **Frecuencia y timing.** 4–5 posts de feed por semana (no uno por dia — consistencia no volumen), stories diarias de 1–3 slides. Hora optima para audiencia USA oeste/mañana: publicar feed entre 9–11am PT. Stories a lo largo del dia. Dias de menor competencia en el algoritmo body jewelry: martes, miercoles y jueves.

- **Mix de formatos.** Distribucion en 30 dias: 10 posts de producto (single image o 2-3 slides), 12 carousels (educativos, journal, storytelling), 4 reels (hooks en camara, 25–35s grabados con iPhone), 4 stories highlight series (no efimeras — para anclar en highlights). Proporcion de contenido: 40% producto, 30% proceso/behind-the-scenes, 20% educativo/aftercare, 10% voz de marca/manifiesto.

### Carousel A — "Aftercare 101: First 30 Days" (Dia 16)

Formato: 8 slides. Fondo oscuro (Obsidian #080706), texto en Bone (#F2EDE4). Logotipo pequeño en corner inferior derecho de cada slide.

**Slide 1 — Portada**
Titulo grande: "Aftercare 101"
Subtitulo: "The first 30 days with a new piercing or stretched lobe."
Eyebrow: "WENU MAPU · CARE GUIDE"

**Slide 2 — Los primeros 2 dias**
Titulo: "Days 1–2: The foundation."
Cuerpo: "Clean twice a day with sterile saline solution — 0.9% sodium chloride, no additives. Do not rotate the jewelry. Do not touch unless cleaning. The less contact, the better."

**Slide 3 — Lo que debes evitar**
Titulo: "What to avoid — always."
Lista:
"Alcohol-based solutions. Hydrogen peroxide. Antibacterial soaps. Rotating the jewelry. Sleeping directly on the piercing. Pool water and ocean water in the first 30 days."

**Slide 4 — Dias 3 a 14**
Titulo: "Days 3–14: The adjustment."
Cuerpo: "Some redness and tenderness is normal. Lymph fluid — thin, clear or pale yellow — is also normal. It is not pus. Pus is thick, opaque, and accompanied by heat. If you see pus, contact your piercer."

**Slide 5 — Materiales que ayudan**
Titulo: "Material matters during healing."
Cuerpo: "If your jewelry is causing prolonged irritation, check the material first. Implant-grade titanium (ASTM F136) and sterling silver 950 are the materials we recommend for healing tissue. Avoid plated metals."

**Slide 6 — Semanas 3 y 4**
Titulo: "Weeks 3–4: Stabilization."
Cuerpo: "Redness should be mostly gone. Tenderness only under pressure. Continue saline once a day. You can now introduce gentle movement — but do not force rotation. The piercing tells you when it is ready."

**Slide 7 — Cuando llamar al profesional**
Titulo: "When to contact your piercer."
Lista:
"Increasing pain after day 3 (not decreasing). Thick discharge with heat. Jewelry that sinks into the tissue. Extreme swelling that does not reduce."
Cuerpo: "When in doubt, go back to your piercer. We can also help via DM or aftercare@wenumapuonline.com."

**Slide 8 — Cierre**
Titulo: "Your body knows."
Cuerpo: "Every body heals at its own pace. The 30-day guide is a baseline — your lobe, your septum, your cartilage may take longer. Listen to what your body signals, not just what the calendar says."
Footer: "Full care guide at new.wenumapuonline.com — link in bio."
