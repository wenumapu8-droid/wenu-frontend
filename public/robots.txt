# Policy: CITATION YES, TRAINING NO.
#
# The distinction that matters, because most crawler lists blur it:
#
#   SEARCH / CITATION crawlers index the site so an AI assistant can cite it
#   and send a real person here. That is discovery. We want it.
#
#   TRAINING crawlers absorb the imagery, copy and craft into a model. The
#   work does not come back and cannot be withdrawn. We decline it.
#
#   USER-TRIGGERED fetchers load a page because a human asked their assistant
#   to open it. That is the human reading our site through another window.
#   Allowed — refusing it only breaks the page for someone who asked for it.
#
# Blocking a training crawler does NOT remove us from that company's search
# product. Most notably: Google-Extended governs Gemini training and Gemini-app
# grounding; it does NOT affect Google Search ranking or AI Overviews, which
# use Googlebot. Blocking it costs us nothing in Search.
#
# Revised 2026-10-10. Previously ClaudeBot was blocked while Anthropic's
# search-side agents were unlisted (so they fell through to the Allow below,
# by accident rather than decision). They are now listed explicitly, which is
# the same outcome stated on purpose.
