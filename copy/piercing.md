## PROCESO PASO A PASO

### EN

**Section label:** How it works

**Step 1 — Book**
Choose your slot on Calendly. You will receive a confirmation email immediately. 24 hours before your appointment, you will receive a reminder with pre-appointment instructions.

**Step 2 — The day before**
Eat something in the two hours before your session. Arrive with the area clean — no makeup, no creams, no lotion. If you have allergies or are on medication that affects healing or bleeding, let us know when you arrive. Bring valid ID. If you are under 18, come with a parent or guardian.

**Step 3 — The session**
When you arrive, we talk through placement, jewelry selection, and any questions. The piercing itself takes a few minutes. The conversation around it takes as long as it needs to.

We use single-use, sterile, professional-grade needles. No guns. Every tool that touches your skin is new or autoclave-sterilized.

**Step 4 — Jewelry**
The jewelry tray carries 8 curated pieces from the Wenu Mapu catalog — mix of T1 ($25–$45) and T2 ($45–$85) pieces. You can also bring your own jewelry if it meets implant-grade standards (implant-grade titanium ASTM F136, implant-grade steel ASTM F138, or solid 14k+ gold). We check it before placement. If it does not meet standards, we will tell you honestly.

**Step 5 — Aftercare briefing**
Before you leave, you get a verbal aftercare rundown and the full written guide sent to your email that day. The guide covers the first 6 weeks. Day 14, you will receive a follow-up email.

**Step 6 — Follow-up**
At day 14, an email arrives checking in. If something looks off before that, you can reach Marimari directly by email. Response time: within 24 hours.

## AFTERCARE — RESUMEN

### EN

**Section label:** Aftercare basics

**Headline:** Your body knows how to heal. Give it the right conditions.

**Do:**
- Clean once daily with sterile saline solution or unscented, alcohol-free soap.
- Pat dry with disposable paper — not cloth.
- Leave it alone. Do not rotate, twist, or change the jewelry.

**Do not:**
- Alcohol, peroxide, or aggressive antibacterial products.
- Pools, hot tubs, or open water for the first 4 weeks.
- Remove the original jewelry before your scheduled change date.

**Healing timelines (approximate):**

| Placement | Healing time |
|---|---|
| Nostril / septum | 6 – 8 weeks |
| Lobe | 6 – 8 weeks |
| Helix / cartilage | 6 – 12 months |
| Daith / conch | 6 – 12 months |
| Industrial | 6 – 12 months |

When to contact us: prolonged swelling past day 5, sharp pain after the first week, yellow or green discharge. Redness and mild swelling in the first 3–4 days is normal.

**Full guide:** wenumapuonline.com/aftercare

## FAQS

### EN

**Section label:** Questions

---

**Does it hurt?**
Depends on placement and individual threshold. Cartilage piercings involve more pressure than lobe piercings. The needle pass is fast. The anticipation is usually the harder part. We talk through it before we start.

---

**What materials are used?**
Single-use, sterile, professional-grade piercing needles. For initial jewelry: implant-grade titanium (ASTM F136), implant-grade steel (ASTM F138), or solid 14k+ gold. These are the only materials considered body-safe for initial piercings. We do not use acrylic, mystery alloys, or plated metals for fresh piercings.

---

**Can I bring my own jewelry?**
Yes, if it meets implant-grade standards. If you are unsure, bring it and we will assess it before your session. We will not place jewelry that does not meet safety standards, and we will explain why.

---

**Can I get pierced with a piece from the Wenu Mapu catalog?**
Yes. We carry a curated selection of 8 pieces at every session. Check the catalog in advance if you have a specific piece in mind — stock is limited. DM @wenu__mapu or email to confirm availability.

---

**How long does the appointment take?**
Sessions are booked in 1-hour slots. The piercing itself takes a few minutes. The rest is conversation, jewelry selection, and aftercare briefing.

---

**Do you pierce minors?**
Yes, with a parent or legal guardian present and with a signed consent form. The guardian must be present for the entire session.

---

**What if I need to cancel?**
Cancel or reschedule at least 12 hours before your slot via Calendly. Late cancellations free the slot for someone else — please use them.

---

**Do you offer any discounts?**
Returning clients receive a 10% discount code (WENU15) at the 14-day follow-up, valid for their next piercing or jewelry purchase for 30 days. We do not run flash sales or group discount events.
