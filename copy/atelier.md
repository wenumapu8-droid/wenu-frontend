## HISTORIA — THE FORMATION

### EN

**Section label:** Formation

**Headline:** Not inheritance. Formation.

**Body:**

Marimari — the voice and face of Wenu Mapu — is Chilean. Not Mapuche by lineage. That distinction is stated here, clearly, once, so it does not need to be guessed.

The craft comes from a specific place: La Ruka de La Cisterna, in the southern end of Santiago. A community space that kept alive the tradition of the rütrafe — the Mapuche silversmith — through workshops open to anyone willing to learn with rigor. Marimari entered those workshops, worked beside rütrafes, learned to raise metal, to read its resistance, to understand the difference between decoration and meaning.

That formation is the foundation of the Atelier. Not a brand story. Not a mystical origin. A specific set of skills, taught by specific people, in a specific place — and a commitment to acknowledge that every time a piece leaves the workshop.

## DIFERENCIACIÓN DE TIERS — ATELIER

### EN

**Section label:** Two ways a piece becomes Atelier

---

**ATELIER-FORGED — by Ocin**

This is the forge work. Sterling silver raised from sheet or wire, hammered, filed, textured, finished by hand at the bench. No casting. No molds. Every piece is a one-of-one.

Each Atelier-Forged piece carries:
- Maker's mark from Ocin
- Material certificate (sterling silver grade, any stone or organic component documented)
- Certificate of the specific batch or series it belongs to

Price range: $85 – $300+ depending on complexity and material.
Production is slow and intentional. When a piece is done, it is listed here. There is no waitlist for stock that does not exist yet — but there is a way to commission directly. See [custom orders].

---

**ATELIER-ASSEMBLED — by Ocin**

Assembly work is not forge work, and we do not present it as such. Atelier-Assembled pieces are composed by Ocin from components sourced from verified suppliers — implant-grade titanium hardware, surgical steel findings, certified organic materials — combined and finished by hand.

The distinction matters because the skill set is different. Assembly requires precision, knowledge of materials, and body-safe standards. It does not require a forge. Both are valid. Neither is the other.

Atelier-Assembled pieces carry:
- Ocin's assembly mark (distinct from the forge mark)
- Material specs for each component
- Body-safe grade certification where applicable

Price range: $45 – $120.

---

### ES

**Etiqueta de sección:** Dos caminos para que una pieza sea Atelier

---

**ATELIER-FORGED — por Ocin**

Esto es trabajo de forja. Plata de ley alzada desde lámina o hilo, martillada, limada, texturizada, terminada a mano en el banco de trabajo. Sin fundición. Sin moldes. Cada pieza es una en su especie.

Cada pieza Atelier-Forged lleva:
- Marca de autor de Ocin
- Certificado de material (grado de plata de ley, cualquier piedra o componente orgánico documentado)
- Certificado del lote o serie específica a la que pertenece

Rango de precio: $85 – $300+ según complejidad y material.
La producción es lenta y deliberada. Cuando una pieza está lista, se publica acá. No hay lista de espera para stock que no existe todavía — pero sí hay una vía para encargar directamente. Ver [pedidos custom].

---

**ATELIER-ASSEMBLED — por Ocin**

El trabajo de ensamblaje no es trabajo de forja, y no lo presentamos como tal. Las piezas Atelier-Assembled las compone Ocin a partir de componentes de proveedores verificados — titanio grado implante, cierres de acero quirúrgico, materiales orgánicos certificados — combinados y terminados a mano.

La distinción importa porque el conjunto de habilidades es diferente. El ensamblaje requiere precisión, conocimiento de materiales y estándares body-safe. No requiere una forja. Ambos son válidos. Ninguno es el otro.

Las piezas Atelier-Assembled llevan:
- Marca de ensamblaje de Ocin (distinta de la marca de forja)
- Especificación de material de cada componente
- Certificación de grado body-safe donde aplica

Rango de precio: $45 – $120.

### EN

**Note (small type, honest):**
Not everything in the Wenu Mapu catalog is Atelier work. The Curated by Marimari line is sourced, not made. It meets the same material and aesthetic standards — but it comes from verified suppliers, not the forge. Both lines are clearly labeled throughout the site. If a product page does not carry an Atelier badge, it is Curated. That is not a lesser category. It is a different one.
