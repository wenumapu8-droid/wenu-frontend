## 11. When to escalate to a real (non-template) reply

The templates are starting points. Replace them with a personal reply when:

- The customer is asking about a piece they already own (they deserve specifics, not boilerplate).
- The customer is in active healing complications (use the medical-disclaimer language but write the rest by hand).
- The customer wrote a thoughtful 2+ paragraph message — match their length and care.
- The customer is a wholesale / press / partnership lead that you actually want to engage (read carefully; reply with specifics).
- The customer is a returning name you recognize (acknowledge them, drop the template formality).

Template = baseline. Personal = preferred. The Wenu Mapu voice is direct and intentional — don't let the template flatten it.
