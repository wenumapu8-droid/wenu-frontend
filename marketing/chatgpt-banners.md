## REGLA CRÍTICA ANTES DE USAR ESTOS PROMPTS

Estos prompts generan **fondos editoriales, atmósferas y composiciones de header**.
NO generan piezas de joyería.

La regla canónica de Wenu Mapu es:
- Fotos de pieza: siempre reales, tomadas por Marimari o fotógrafo en el taller.
- IA: solo para fondos, atmósferas, texturas, composiciones sin joyería específica.

Nunca publicar una imagen IA diciendo que es la joya real. Si el banner lleva joya, es composición real (foto de pieza encima de fondo IA). Si el banner es solo atmósfera, puede ser 100% IA.

Formato recomendado de salida: 1440×600px (banner web) o 1080×1920px (Story). Indicar en el prompt.

Paleta de referencia:
- Negro cósmico: #0A0A0F
- Hueso cálido: #F0EDE8
- Oro Wenu: #C9A84C (aproximación — validar contra logo real)
- Tierra profunda: #6F4E37
- Arena noble: #D6C1A3

## PROMPT 1 — Atacama Meteorite Series

**Colección:** Atacama Meteorite Series
**Uso:** Header de página de colección / Banner de drop en IG

**Prompt (pegar directamente en ChatGPT / DALL-E 3):**

Create a wide editorial header image for a fine body jewelry brand. The scene is an abstract landscape referencing the Atacama Desert of northern Chile at night. The palette is: deep black (#0A0A0F), warm bone white (#F0EDE8), and muted gold (#C9A84C) as the only accent. The composition shows a vast, dark, mineral surface — cracked dry earth or iron-rich desert stone — under a clear night sky with a sparse, precise constellation pattern. No figures. No jewelry rendered in the image. The texture of the ground should be macro and geological, as if seen from very close: the pattern of a meteorite's surface (Widmanstätten structure — interlocking crystalline lines in muted metallic tones) fading into the desert floor. The mood is ancient, cosmological, and precise — not mystical or decorative. No glow effects, no lens flares, no bokeh. Flat light from above, very slight directional shadow. The image must function as a clean background for text overlay: leave the central third of the image relatively uncluttered. Style reference: editorial photography for a European design brand, not astrology or new-age wellness. Aspect ratio 16:9 for web header use. No text in the image.

## PROMPT 2 — Atelier by Ocin

**Colección:** Atelier by Ocin (Forged + Assembled)
**Uso:** Header de página Atelier / Posts de drop

**Prompt (pegar directamente en ChatGPT / DALL-E 3):**

Create a wide editorial header image for a handcraft body jewelry atelier. The scene is an extreme close-up of a silversmith's workshop bench — but abstracted, not photojournalistic. The palette is: near-black (#0A0A0F), warm bone (#F0EDE8), and burnished sterling silver tones (cool grey with warm undertones). The composition should suggest the textures of craft without showing any specific jewelry piece: the grain of a worn wooden bench surface, the edge of a metal file, abstract reflections of silver sheet metal, residue of metal filings catching a single source of directional light. The light source is a single workshop lamp from the upper left — warm filament tone. No human hands visible. No tools recognizable as specific tools. The image is texture and materiality, not documentation. Mood: slow, intentional, analog. The craft is serious and unhurried. No romantic soft-focus. No dust motes. No warmth-filtered vintage effect. Sharp where the material is sharp. The image must work as a background for minimal text overlay in Cinzel typeface (white or bone tone). Aspect ratio 16:9 for web header. No text in the image.

## PROMPT 3 — Curated by Marimari

**Colección:** Curated by Marimari
**Uso:** Header de sección Curated en catálogo / Posts de piezas seleccionadas

**Prompt (pegar directamente en ChatGPT / DALL-E 3):**

Create a wide editorial header image for a curated body jewelry collection. The concept is curation as a deliberate act — selection, not accumulation. The composition shows an arrangement of material swatches or surfaces in abstract form: implant-grade titanium anodized in neutral tones (silver, light champagne, matte black), small sections of sustainably sourced walnut wood grain, and polished surgical steel surface, arranged with deliberate spacing on a warm bone-toned (#F0EDE8) background. Nothing decorative. Each material is presented as if it is being evaluated before a decision. The light is flat, even, from above — like a photographer's white box, but warmer. The mood is editorial and discerning, not minimalist-corporate. No jewelry shapes. No gemstones rendered. No flowers, no botanicals, no lifestyle props. The image should feel like a materials archive from a design studio. Aspect ratio 16:9 for web header. No text in the image.

## PROMPT 4 — Saturday Piercing Truckee

**Servicio:** Saturday Piercing Truckee
**Uso:** Header de página de piercing / Stories de sábado / Promo de booking

**Prompt (pegar directamente en ChatGPT / DALL-E 3):**

Create a vertical editorial image (9:16 ratio, for Instagram Story format) for a private piercing service in Truckee, California. The scene is abstract and clinical in the right way: a close-up of a sterile, organized setup on a clean dark surface — but depicted abstractly, not as product photography. Suggest the following without showing them literally: the sealed packaging of a single-use sterile needle (just the abstract shape of a sealed pouch, no text), a small dark ceramic or steel tray with one or two abstract metallic circular shapes (not recognizable as specific jewelry), a single pair of black nitrile gloves laid flat. The palette is: deep dark background (#0A0A0F), warm bone accent (#F0EDE8) for the gloves or surface detail, and a single accent of muted gold (#C9A84C) for one metallic element. The mood is: calm, precise, trustworthy. This is not a horror or edgy aesthetic. It is the aesthetic of a skilled practitioner who takes their work seriously. No blood, no wound imagery, no shock. The image must leave the upper third clear for text overlay. No text in the image. Style reference: Scandinavian medical design aesthetics meets minimal craft studio.

## PROMPT 5 — Collab Artists

**Línea:** Collab / Artist collaborations
**Uso:** Header de sección Collab / Anuncio de colaboraciones

**Prompt (pegar directamente en ChatGPT / DALL-E 3):**

Create a wide editorial header image for an artist collaboration program within a body jewelry brand rooted in Chilean silversmithing and Mapuche cultural tradition. The concept is two distinct vocabularies meeting at a single material point. The composition shows two abstract surface areas side by side, separated by a thin vertical line: on the left, a surface that reads as hand-worked sterling silver — hammered texture, irregular and warm; on the right, a surface that could be graphic, print, or painted — flat, geometric, high contrast, suggesting a different artistic discipline (printmaking, weaving, illustration). The palette: left side uses silver and bone tones (#F0EDE8, cool silver); right side uses a single strong accent that contrasts — either deep terracotta or a saturated teal. The dividing line between the two sides is precise and clean — a seam, not a blur. The mood is collaborative but not merged: two distinct things in conversation, maintaining their own integrity. No human figures. No specific cultural symbols used as decoration. Aspect ratio 16:9 for web header. No text in the image.

## NOTAS DE USO PARA DAVID (EL DISEÑADOR)

1. Estos prompts generan la atmósfera / fondo. El texto (wordmark, headline) se superpone en post-producción usando la paleta y tipografía del brand kit.
2. Si DALL-E 3 interpreta el prompt demasiado literalmente y renderiza joyas específicas, regenerar con la instrucción adicional: "Do not render any jewelry pieces. Background and texture only."
3. Para composición final: wordmark Wenu Mapu (Cinzel Bold, color hueso #F0EDE8 o gold #C9A84C) en el tercio superior o inferior de la imagen. El copy va en el espacio libre indicado en el prompt.
4. Si el banner va a ser usado como Story de IG, ajustar el ratio a 9:16 y especificarlo en el prompt — cada prompt tiene la relación de aspecto indicada.
5. Midjourney alternativo: los prompts funcionan en Midjourney con la adición de "--ar 16:9 --v 6 --style raw" al final. Quitar las instrucciones de aspecto del cuerpo del prompt si se usa el flag de Midjourney.
