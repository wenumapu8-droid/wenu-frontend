## Research admission rule

For every source record capture:

- title / author / institution or publisher / year;
- DOI or canonical URL;
- source type;
- evidence class;
- exact scope;
- what it supports;
- what it does **not** support;
- KODEX design transduction;
- implementation relevance;
- license / cultural provenance.

A source does not become KODEX canon simply because it is scientifically valid or visually attractive.

## Biofield / toroidal claim audit

### `BIOFIELD-01` — magnetocardiography

**Evidence direction:** cardiac electrical activity produces very weak magnetic fields measurable with magnetocardiography / magnetic sensors.

**KODEX use:** establish the narrow scientific fact before any symbolic translation.

**Does not establish:** an exact universal body-spanning toroidal aura, emotional information transfer through such a field, or interpersonal energetic communication.

### `BIOFIELD-02` — HeartMath claim source

HeartMath is recorded as a **claim/reference source** for statements appearing in adjacent spiritual/wellness literature. It is not automatically treated as scientific authority.

Required audit:

1. extract exact claim;
2. identify cited experiment;
3. identify sensor/method;
4. locate independent peer-reviewed support or replication;
5. separate measurement from extrapolation;
6. classify `VERIFIED`, `INFERRED`, `SPECULATIVE`, or `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` claim-by-claim.

- perceptual optimization of dense visualizations;
- typography legibility at small technical-display sizes;
- temporal hierarchy / motion perception;
- density and negative-space metrics that are operational rather than aesthetic slogans.

### Computational graphics

- ray marching;

- LOD / adaptive render tiers;

### Historical / symbolic geometry

Do not research “sacred geometry” as one flattened doctrine. Build separate provenance tracks:

- Classical Greek mathematical/cosmological texts and later interpretation;
- Islamic geometric construction and object/architectural provenance;
- historical architectural proportion systems;
- named esoteric schools using their own documents;
- Indigenous geometric/symbolic systems only through culture-specific sources, authority and rights frameworks.
