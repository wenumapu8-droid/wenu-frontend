**EN:**

This piece began as a sheet of sterling silver. No casting, no mold — the metal was cut, annealed, and bent by hand in Ocin's workshop on May 18, 2026: the first day back in the atelier after four years away from the bench.

Ocin is the workshop name of Marimari, trained as a silversmith in the rütrafe tradition at the Ruka de La Cisterna, Santiago. The rütrafe is the Mapuche silversmith — a technical lineage that runs from the chaway (hanging earring) to the trarilonko (headband), each piece made by someone who knows what comes before it. Marimari is not Mapuche by lineage. He was formed in that tradition and carries it with full awareness of the distance between practitioner and origin.

The septum was formed in that distance: with respect, with discipline, with the hands.

Sterling silver (925). Form follows the natural fall of the nose. No stone setting on this piece — the metal is the work. Finished with fine sandpaper, then polished on cloth. The surface will show wear over time; that wear is not damage.
