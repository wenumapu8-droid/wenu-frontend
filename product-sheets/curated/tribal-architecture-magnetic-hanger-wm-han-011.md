## Long Description

**EN:**

An ear hanger with a magnetic closure and tribal-geometric surface patterning. The magnetic closure is the functional differentiator: it opens and closes without threading, without tools, without the fumbling that comes with most hanger closures. You place the two halves, they meet, they hold.

The surface pattern is geometric — angular, structured, architectural in the sense that it suggests a building's rhythm rather than an organic form. It is tribal in that it draws from a long tradition of symmetrical, geometric body adornment across cultures.

I selected this piece because the magnetic closure expands who can wear a hanger: people with limited dexterity in their hands, people who change ear jewelry frequently, anyone who finds the standard CBR or segment ring closure difficult. The design supports the function.

Sold as pair.
