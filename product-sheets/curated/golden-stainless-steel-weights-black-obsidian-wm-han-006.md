**EN:**

Ear weights in gold-tone stainless steel with a black obsidian stone setting. Obsidian is volcanic glass — it forms when lava cools rapidly, trapping gas before it can escape, leaving a solid black with a conchoidal fracture and a surface that reads as depth, not just color.

The gold-tone setting frames the obsidian without competing with it. The metal is stainless steel with a warm-tone finish — durable, body-safe, consistent in color.

I selected this piece because the gold-black combination is one of the most coherent in body jewelry. It reads in any context: minimal wardrobe, layered look, formal or daily. The weight appropriate for stretched lobes; the visual is anything but subtle.
