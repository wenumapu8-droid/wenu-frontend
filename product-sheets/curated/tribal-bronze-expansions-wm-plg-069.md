## Long Description

**EN:**

Ear expansions in bronze with tribal patterning on the surface. Bronze is one of the oldest body-adornment metals — it predates silver in most metalworking traditions, and it carries the weight and warmth that silver and steel do not.

The tribal patterning here is geometric: angular, repeated, consistent across the surface. It draws from a visual tradition that spans body adornment across the Pacific, the Americas, and sub-Saharan Africa — not claimed as specific to any one culture, but shaped by that collective language.

Bronze develops a patina with wear. The surface darkens slightly where it contacts skin — that is the metal working. It can be polished back if you prefer the brighter surface; it can be left to darken if you prefer the aged read.

Sold as pair. Specify gauge at checkout.
