## Long Description

**EN:**

Saddle-style ear expansions with opal inlay. The saddle form sits flush in an expanded lobe — it does not protrude forward or hang; it rests exactly where the lobe ends.

The opal in this piece is simulated — the color shift is produced by a layered material, not a natural mined stone. I want to be clear about that because opal is a stone that gets misrepresented often: natural opal is opaque to translucent, expensive, and fragile. Simulated opal produces a similar visual at a fraction of the cost, in a setting that can withstand daily wear.

I selected this piece because the proportions are right, the color shift is consistent across the surface, and the saddle form fits cleanly in a stretched lobe without adding unnecessary bulk.

Available in standard gauges. Specify at checkout.
